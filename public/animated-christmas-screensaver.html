A few things to know

What is included?

One digital Christmas experience featuring the animated Christmas window, a collection of playful scenes and the option to prepare a personal message from Santa. The short video above shows a sample of the actual animations.

Is this an automatic computer screensaver?

It is an interactive Christmas experience that plays in your browser. It does not install itself as your computer’s automatic idle screensaver.

How do I access it after buying?

Use the access button after your payment is confirmed. Keep using the same browser and device. An email recovery option is not available yet; contact Ronald if you need help with access.

Can I watch on my phone, laptop or PC?

Yes, use a modern browser with JavaScript enabled. The preview lets you try playback on your device before purchasing. A landscape screen gives the window the most room.

Can I change the message?

Yes. Prepare names and your own text before starting the message scene. Your message stays in your browser on that device.
