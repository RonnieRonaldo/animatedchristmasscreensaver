A few things to know

What is included, and how long does it play?

A Christmas loop lasting about 7 minutes 37 seconds repeats before and after your personal surprise. The special series lasts about 3 minutes 42 seconds, or up to 4 minutes 12 seconds for a longer message.

How do I start the personal surprise?

Enter names and your message, then open the Christmas window. When the kids are ready, press Start your Christmas surprise. The loop pauses, the special series plays, and the loop resumes automatically afterwards. The message stays readable for 30–60 seconds, with a little hourglass.

Can I change the names and message?

Yes. Use Show controls → Edit message to prepare a new surprise. Your text stays in your browser on that device.

Is this an automatic computer screensaver?

It plays in your browser; it does not install as an automatic idle screensaver. Keep the playback page open and your device awake.

How do I return after buying?

Bookmark My access and return in the same browser on the same device. Keep its cookies. Email recovery is not available yet; contact Ronald if you need help.

How can I watch on my TV?

Connect a laptop to your TV with an HDMI cable and select that HDMI input. For an iPhone or iPad, use Screen Mirroring with an AirPlay-compatible TV or Apple TV. Other phones, tablets and laptops need a compatible screen-mirroring method.

For a Fire TV Stick, check that both devices support mirroring; iPhones and iPads do not work with its standard Miracast mirroring. Use the same Wi-Fi for wireless mirroring. Mirror the whole screen so the personal message appears too. Test your setup with the preview before buying.
